[b][size=4]What it is and why use it[/size][/b]

Division HUD adds an extra combat HUD styled after the status readout in Tom Clancy's The Division. It overlays a compact, positionable strip showing your core vitals (toughness, health, stamina/dodge), your resource and loadout slots (weapon, ammo, grenade, pocket stimm, active buffs), and your combat ability with an icon and cooldown timer.

On top of the bars it layers situational-awareness tools: an enemy target readout with enemy type and active debuffs, an approach/alerts feed (bosses, specialists, threats, mission objectives, downed teammates), a nearby-pickup proximity detector, a danger-zone warning for hazards, and a Tactical Advisor that flashes relevant resources and warns on low ammo, health, wounds, grenades, or high corruption.

It can auto-show or hide with the camera perspective, drift dynamically with view movement, suppress redundant vanilla HUD elements, and hook into companion mods (Custom HUD, StimmCountdown, RecolorStimms).

[size=4][b]Installation (unofficial build)[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]1. Uninstall/disable original [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/813]Division HUD[/url] mod.
2. Install and enable [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/8]Darktide Mod Framework[/url].
3. Install this archive with Vortex, or extract it into <game>\mods\ so you get <game>\mods\DivisionHUD\.
4. Make sure "DivisionHUD" is listed in <game>\mods\mod_load_order.txt.[/color]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/8]Darktide Mod Framework[/url]. Custom HUD, StimmCountdown, and RecolorStimms are optional and only used if you have them installed.[/color]

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]NVIDIA DSR / supersampling above your monitor's native resolution can push the HUD off-screen. Fix: set the in-game Resolution to native (use DLSS, not DSR).[/color]
