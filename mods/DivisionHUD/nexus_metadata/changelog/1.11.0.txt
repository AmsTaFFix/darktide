Community fixes by LucLeto.
Fixed the "attempt to call method 'remaining_ability_cooldown' (a nil value)" crash after the game's 1.13 patch, which replaced ability cooldowns with a regenerating resource. The combat-ability bar and ability icon now read charge and cooldown state from the new ability resource system, mirroring the vanilla ability HUD.
Fixed the crash when entering the Meat Grinder, shooting range, or tutorial. The HUD no longer injects itself into those training-ground screens.
Fixed tracked-deployable timers (Adamant blitz drone, Broker stimm field) for the patch's projectile and talent changes, so they register and count down again.
