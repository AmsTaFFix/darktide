[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]The Dueling Sword has a speed tech: sprint, throw an attack, slide right as the attack's move-speed curve peaks, and the slide locks in that boosted velocity instead of a plain sprint-slide's own (slower) speed. The window to hit is well under half a second and timed off your own current speed, not a fixed count - too fast and finicky to reliably hit by hand, so this mod does it for you, every cycle, reading your real speed and attack state live and reacting to it.

It also chains: once one slide ends, it starts the next attack right at the tail of it instead of waiting for you to rebuild a full sprint, so the boosted cycle repeats back to back for as long as you keep holding sprint forward.[/color]

[size=4][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]1. Install and enable [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/8]Darktide Mod Framework[/url].
2. Install this archive with Vortex, or extract it into <game>\mods\ so you get <game>\mods\DuelingSwordSpeedTech\.
3. Make sure "DuelingSwordSpeedTech" is listed in <game>\mods\mod_load_order.txt.[/color]

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[color=#D4D4D8]- [b]Light technique[/b] (recommended, default): releases the windup almost instantly - no charge needed - then slides straight out of sprinting. Measured faster on average than plain sprinting, and the cycle costs little to no net stamina since the slide itself lets stamina regenerate.
- [b]Heavy technique[/b] (experimental): charges and releases a heavy for a bigger single peak, then force-drops the sprint input to open the slide. Hits a higher peak speed than Light, but the weapon's own minimum charge time (0.375-0.5s, fixed by the game) drags its average below plain sprinting in testing so far. Left in and documented for players who want to help tune it further - see its own options section and feel free to report findings.
- Chain-next-attack-during-glide: keeps the boosted cycle going back to back instead of resetting to a full sprint each time.
- Tunable sliders for slide-peak detection, glide duration, charge/slide timing overrides, and a stamina safety floor.
- A debug trace tool (keybind) that dumps the last ~600 frames of cycle data to a file, for anyone who wants to tune the sliders from real numbers instead of guesswork.[/color]
